# Episode V2 — Signing in and a tour of the screen: narration draft

Written part: [Part 2](../02-sign-in-and-find-your-way.md). About three minutes. Each `[SLIDE]` line names the image on
screen while the lines under it are read. This is a draft for the episode kit, not the final script.

---

[SLIDE: title — "Signing in and a tour of the screen". Under it: "By the end, you can sign in and say what each area
of the screen is for."]

The next weekly drop is coming. Before anything else, Peter needs to get in.

[SLIDE: S02 — the sign-in panel]

Open your Resource Explorer's address in a browser.
The sign-in panel asks for your Egeria user id and password.
The label under the title says experimental. It means what it says: Resource Explorer is still changing, in
response to what its users ask for.
Type Peter's user id and password, and click Sign in.
There is no separate account. Egeria checks the password, and Resource Explorer does not keep it.

[SLIDE: S03 — Peter's screen, plain]

This is Peter's screen. Peter's user id is at the top right, next to Sign out.
From now on, everything you do is recorded under that id.

[SLIDE: S03 labelled — reveal callout 1]

Along the top is the header. Activity and Admin are here, along with your user id.

[SLIDE: reveal callout 2]

Under it is the strip. Scouting to Curate, numbered one to six, are the stages a data source goes through, left to
right. The next episodes walk through them.
At the right end are requests for action, things a person must settle, and Chat.

[SLIDE: reveal callout 3]

The row of perspectives narrows the questions to the ones a perspective cares about, such as Security or Data Owner.
With none chosen, you see every question.

[SLIDE: reveal callout 4]

On the left is the sidebar: repositories, databases, file systems, and open data. This is where you add a data
source, and find it again.

[SLIDE: reveal callout 5]

In the middle is the data source you are looking at, with its questions and surveys.

[SLIDE: reveal callout 6]

And on the right is chat, scoped to that data source. Chat in the strip shows or hides it.

[SLIDE: S04 — the Activity panel]

Click Activity to see what has run, latest first. Each entry says what ran, on which data source, and whether it
worked. Use error to see only what failed.

[SLIDE: closing — "Next: adding a database, a file system and a repository". "What can go wrong: see part 2 of the
written tutorial."]

One thing to know: a sign-in lasts about an hour. When it ends, sign in again. Nothing you saved is lost.
Next, Peter adds the trial's three sources.
