An investigation says why; its scope says which; a work list is the bench

Three words, one thing each. Scope is already the word on screen everywhere except the investigation page's “Members”. Work list keeps its name and means only the bench (one resource type, rows × questions, narrowed by shortlist). The report act that says “add to work list” — the owner's trigger — becomes “add to investigation”. Values are illustrative.

Scope and this investigation's lists sit under the selector. Other lists fold to one line. Suggestions are not work lists.
