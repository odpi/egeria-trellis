Two kinds of “new”. A new schema waits for your Publish, matched by a rule or not. A new table in a schema you published is added by Egeria’s cataloguer on its own refresh: that’s Egeria’s current default, and the row says it in Egeria’s name. To hold a schema’s new tables back, leave the schema out; Egeria can’t take table lists yet (S1, S11).
