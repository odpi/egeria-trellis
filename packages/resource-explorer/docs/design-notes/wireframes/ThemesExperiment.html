Resource Explorer in the Portal's look: an experiment

The same screen four times, with identical words and layout: Analysis, Survey & analyses, on coco_pharma. Only the look changes. Rendering 2 changes just one thing from RE today, the typeface of the body and detail text, to separate the effect of the typeface from the effect of the color scheme. Renderings 3 and 4 take the Portal's whole look: typefaces, colors, card treatment, corner radius and number tiles.

Look first at the detail text: the run line, the provenance lines under each card, the answer paragraph and the analysis rows. That's the text you found hard to read.

1 · RE today: dark chrome, paper content, Cormorant Garamond headings, Lora body
2 · RE today with one change: body and detail text in the system sans (headings, labels and palette unchanged)
3 · The Portal's look, dark: navy ground, bordered cards, system sans, big-number tiles
4 · The Portal's look, light: the same treatment on its light palette
